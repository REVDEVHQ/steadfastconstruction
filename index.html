FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our Seattle construction services.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, Steadfast Construction is fully licensed, bonded, and insured. We carry comprehensive general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage to protect you and our team on every project.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve Seattle and all of King County, including Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland, Renton, Kent, Federal Way, Sammamish, Issaquah, Mercer Island, Shoreline, and Bothell.

How much do your services cost?

Every project is unique, so costs vary based on scope, materials, and complexity. We provide detailed, transparent written estimates at no charge. Contact us for a free consultation and quote.

Do you offer warranties?

Yes! Every project is backed by our comprehensive 5-year workmanship warranty. We stand behind our work and are committed to your complete satisfaction.

How long will my project take?

Timeline depends on project scope. Kitchen remodels typically take 2-4 weeks, bathroom renovations 1-3 weeks, and custom home builds 6-12 months. We'll provide a detailed schedule during your consultation.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

Absolutely. We manage all necessary permits, inspections, and ensure full compliance with Seattle building codes. You won't have to worry about the paperwork—we handle it all.
